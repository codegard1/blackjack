# Table Behavior and Features

* **Offline & PWA**: Install the app from your browser's install menu (or Add to Home Screen on iOS). After the first online visit finishes caching, the game loads and plays offline, including character portraits; cloud sync still needs a connection. Production builds served over HTTPS (or localhost) enable this; `npm start` does not. New versions activate and reload open tabs automatically, preserving the game saved in browser storage; updates are checked on page loads, hourly while open, and when the connection returns.
* **Persistence**: Game state and settings are saved in the browser, so a reload resumes the current game.
* **Players**: Create named players from the Players dialog and choose who plays. Roster changes (the human player and the computer players) never affect a hand in play; between hands the table updates at once, and a change made during a hand is seated when the next hand is dealt. Each player's lifetime statistics (hands, wins, losses, pushes, blackjacks, busts, and net result: the bank change across every hand, shown as `+$` or `−$`) are kept in the browser and shown in the same dialog.
* **Activity Log**: The activity log records each deal, draw, wager, stay, and result. The browser keeps its newest 1,000 entries. It is a drawer that extends from the bottom edge of the table, opened and closed with the icon button at the end of the table's dock; the Options panel's **Open Activity Log by default** switch decides whether it starts open. A new entry slides down into the log once any toast announcing it has had a moment on screen, or right away when an action has no toast or its notifications are switched off.
* **Strategy Hints**: With **Show strategy hints** switched on in the Options panel (off by default), your turn names the textbook play for your hand against the dealer's face-up card, in a line over your buttons (`"Hint: Soft 18 vs dealer 9: hit"`), and the button for it is the filled one. The plays are basic strategy for the table in play: its number of decks, its rule for the dealer on soft 17, and this game's rules (double down on any two cards, none after a split, one split, no surrender). One deck, two decks and a shoe each have their own chart; with one deck, for example, an 8 is doubled against a 5 or 6 and a pair of 7s stands against a 10. A hint falls back when a double down or a split is not available (too many cards, a hand that was already split, or a bank that cannot cover it). It does not count cards. The computer players make the same play as often as their discipline allows, and go on instinct the rest of the time. The charts were worked out by expected value from the cards left in the shoe; `npm run strategy-chart -- 1 S17` prints one (decks, then `S17` or `H17`), and the tests check every cell of all eight.
* **Appearance**: Appearance settings in the Options panel include a light, dark, system or high contrast theme; five accent colors that recolor the Fluent UI theme and the table felt; three card face layouts (classic, minimal, bold); and a card back chosen from five patterns and five colors with a live preview. A **Reset appearance** button restores the defaults. Cards are styled with CSS from the Fluent UI theme tokens, so they follow the chosen theme, and animate onto the table.
* **Hole Card Flip**: When a round ends, the dealer's hole card turns over with a 3D CSS flip (also when **Show Dealer Hand** is switched on). When the reveal ends the round, the dealer's seat stays in view while the card turns and for a second after it, so the hand and the result can be taken in before the table moves on; play waits for all of it like it does for the pot. With reduced motion requested by the system, the card swaps faces at once, with no turn and no wait.
* **Table Layout & Seat Queue**: The table fills the window below the header, with the same margin above it and on its left, right and bottom edges. Seats that are not on the stage wait as name-and-bank chips in the reverse of turn order: the dealer, who plays last, at the top, and the human, who plays first, at the bottom. Seats still to play wait above the stage, the next to play closest to it. The seat in control moves to the stage and expands, and its cards scale to fill their container; clicking a chip brings that seat forward instead. When a seat's turn is over it moves down to just below the stage, so the first to play ends at the bottom. When the round ends nobody has the stage: every seat stays a chip in one column, in the middle on a wide screen, the dealer first and the human last, and the next deal sends them back to their places. As the round's results are told in the toasts, the human seat and the dealer's chip are brought into focus in turn (computer seats still show their results on their chips and in the activity log): each pulses, brightens and takes an outline in its result's color (green for a win, red for a loss, grey for a push). The dealer's chip carries a tag that names it and shows its status (`"Dealer · Waiting"`, `"Dealer · Playing"`, its result). On a wide screen the chips are a column at the side (left by default; the Options panel's **Seat queue side** setting moves it to the right), waiting seats at the top and played seats at the bottom. On a narrow screen the table is one column: the waiting seats, the stage, then the played seats, as compact full-width rows.
* **Dock & Pot**: A dock along the table's bottom edge holds the pot, the game's buttons and the activity log button in one row. Start game, your action buttons, and Next hand and Change players all appear there; while a computer player or the dealer plays, the dock names who is playing instead. The dock stays at the bottom of the window when the table is taller than the screen. The pot stays at the start of the dock, before a game and after a hand ends. Between hands it slides up out of view and the new pot slides in from the top. When money is added to it, it shimmers and bounces. Play waits for these animations to finish.
* **Toast Notifications**: Hand outcomes (mid-round blackjacks and busts, and end-of-round results for your seat and the dealer), pot contributions, sit-outs, and characters leaving to work or returning appear as toast notices over the table; deals, hits, stands, and turn changes stay in the activity log without toasts. A lone wager, double down, or split is announced in the player's name, with their portrait or initials (`"Chris doubled down for $25"`). Start-of-round antes and computer wagers, or several wagers at once, merge into one pot notice (`"+$150 added to the pot"`), and several players sitting out share one notice. Pot contributions have their own queue directly over the pot at the start of the dock and appear straight away without pausing computer play; the pot's badge shows the new amount and a newer line replaces the last. Center-table notices appear in a running feed just above the middle of the dock (up to three lines at once, newest at the bottom and older ones dimmer), fading after a few seconds or when a newer one needs their place; only gameplay outcomes pause computer play while queued. When a round ends, the results for your seat and the dealer are told after the dealer's card has turned, while Next hand waits until the last has been told. The Options panel's **Notifications** section lets you switch **Gameplay notifications** and **Pot notifications** on or off independently without delaying the activity log. Tapping the pot lists what each player has put in so far this hand, one line per player over the pot (`"Chris: $50"`), regardless of notification settings; it goes away after a few seconds, when the pot is tapped again, or when the next hand starts.
